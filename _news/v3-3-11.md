As part of this update, everyone gets one (1) Free Respec, which must be completed (read: finalized and sent to a Staff member) within the week (before 6/22/2026).

Regardless of whether you choose to take the respec or not, unfortunately, we will need to get a new Character Sheet from you, so please grab the latest character sheet from the discord pins (or see [HERE](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1rJ0DwrlNRvlymawxD0DuWz9xvGDX0O_AishSE9C3_jc/edit?gid=2086603970#gid=2086603970)).  You can use the new ticket system to send the link to Staff.

And yes, this does mean that SC can rebuild their clan swords.

#### Kyosha
 - Swordsman Corps' Balance Tassel feature that reduced the speed of Parry and gave it a +3 vs Disarm (replacing the normal -3) was removed; in its place is a new 3-pt feature that allows for any disarm to simply leave your sword hanging from your arm, retrievable as a Speed 1 action.  While the Parry reduction is a loss, overall it should be the reliable Disarm prevention method that the +3 was unsatisfactory for those concerned about such.
 - Swordsman Corps' transform tassel option was changed to Stamina +5, rather than Chakra +5, and had some clarifying text added for how the tag change is handled.
 - Swordsman Corps' Block Interrupt sword feature was increased to Speed 6 (from 4), mostly to make it less overtly a replacement for Defender.
 - Swordsman Corps' clan sword options to utilize CHA in place of DEX for their sword abilities were removed.
 - Swordman Corps' have a new Yin Blade feature that allows you refund AP spent on an attack with your sword when you miss.
 - Swordman Corps' have a new Yin Hilt feature that allows you reduce the sword's base Speed.
 - Yurigami's Paper Clones had 'these function as regular bunshin' .. text added to them just to be consistent with other clone types (in that they inherent the rules of the E-rank general ninjutsu Bunshin technique, aside from their stated differences).

## Combat
 - To reiterate from the hotfix necessary for a security concern: Damage had its clarified "base" versus non-base damage modifiers; those that are "base" have been updated to include that specific wording in their text.  base damage bonus modifiers are affected by multipliers alongside your stat; non-base are additive and non-affecting of one another.  This mostly serves to make unique-based bonuses, and Brute Force / Potent Chakra, slightly more beneficial.
 - Take the Hit is now clear that you must be able to reach the target you are trying to protect.  Shunshin gained a new stipulation to aid in this, below.
 - Delay is explicit in the cost(s) for attacks with Delay having them paid for when it is declared, rather than when it takes place (due to its Delay).

## Abilities
 - Preparation had it added that effects which reduce your actions Speed (such as AP, Celerity, and Seal Master) do not apply to the Speed of effects activated with it.
 - Monkey Grip finally no longer erroneously lists an incorrect damage bonus increase it's simply telling you doesn't apply when dual-wielding two two-handed weapons.

## Jutsu
 - Clone rules (in Jutsu --> Jutsu Rules) got some changes to try and streamline and simplify Clones ever-so-slightly. Namely:
 - Clones do not benefit or suffer from effects that designate or exclude you.
 - Clones now share all consecutive bonuses with you (things such as Tsuga's reductions in cost/speed from multiple uses, Relentless's bonuses, Combo Counters, etc).
 - Clones can not 'stack' the same effects as you on a target: Clones can't Incarnation of Desert (or Grudge Rain) to apply it repeatedly at once, unless the effect itself states it stacks with itself (such as Thundering Strike).

#### Taijutsu
 - Compression Hold (C-rank Grapple) had its Stamina cost increased to 15, and now increased the speed of the defender's Dodge and Parry interrupts by 1 (stackable up to two times, for +4 to each) so long as the Locks you use it with are maintained.
 - Break Grapple, Escape Grapple, and Counter Throw had text to make it clearer how it handles multiple grapplers.
 - Retaliation had it clarified the added dice can at most equal your base dice (to double your dice), not triple.
 - Tentai-ite no Jutsu was reduced from Rank B to Rank C. its Speed was increased to 8, its Upkeep was changed to 2 + X/2 (from 2 + X/3), and its penalty to your Damage Bonus was halved to X/2 (from X).  Its requirement of Weapon Focus: Bow was removed.

#### Ninjutsu 
 - Shunshin (D-rank General) had the Link tag added (it always had that capacity), and is explicit about being able to be linked to Take the Hit in order to utilize that action (via movement according to AP spent).
 - Avenging Lightning God (C-rank Raiton) had its Paralysis now stack and refresh its duration by 10 IC, but had its starting and maximum duration reduced to 30 IC, from 40.  Ideally this simplifies its tracking greatly (one status vs multiple separate instances).

## Equipment
 - Sheath Weapon (the action) makes it clear now that you can not sheath weapons you did not begin the scene with aside from small ranged thrown weapons, unless you have some special means to do so.
 - Melee weapons had some old text trying to clarify what two-handed's +33% applied to; it is no longer needed with 'base' damage bonus codified.

## Talents
 - Nature Chakra's Path to Inner Peace had its requirement of Basic Training 5 removed; it was moved to Sage Mode, instead.
 - Nature Chakra's Sage Mode had its minimum NC stored to enter Sage Mode with halved (from 50 to 25).  This is a bit of an experimental change that may be reverted if it's deemed too strong, but given all Sage Mode benefits are dependent upon your NC counters, perhaps it will make it feel more accessible/less cumbersome.  We shall see in.. another thousand XP or so.
