## Creation
 - 
 - 

#### Kyosha
 - Swordsman Corps' Balance Tassel feature that reduced the speed of Parry and gave it a +3 vs Disarm (replacing the normal -3) was removed; in its place is a new 4-pt feature that allows for any disarm to simply leave your sword hanging from your arm, retrievable as a Speed 1 action.  While the Parry reduction is a loss, overall it should be the reliable Disarm prevention method that the +3 was seemingly *not* for those concerned about such (due to the prevalence of auto-disarm wounds).

## Combat
 - Damage had it clarified "base" versus non-base damage modifiers; those that are "base" have been updated to include that specific wording in their text.  base damage bonus modifiers are affected by multipliers alongside your stat; non-base are additive and non-affecting of one another.  This mostly serves to make unique-based bonuses, and Brute Force / Potent Chakra, slightly more beneficial.

## Jutsu
 - 
 - 

#### Taijutsu
 - Compression Hold (C-rank Grapple) had its Stamina cost increased to 15, and now increased the speed of the defender's Dodge and Parry interrupts by 1 (stackable up to two times, for +4 to each) so long as the Locks you use it with are maintained.
 - 

#### Ninjutsu 
 - Chakra Scalpel's bonus to its damage and wounding based on your medicine ranks no longer applies if the location you hit has armor covering it.
