#### Kyosha
 - Swordman Corps' yang tassel which deals damage after someone parries your sword states explicitly it does not stack with Chakra Flow: Wind and similar such effects.

#### Ninjutsu 
 - Shikoku Fuuin (D-rank Sealing) lost its Chakra cost and had its description modified to no longer allow for permanent scarring.
 - Torii Seal was added as a new C-rank Sealing jutsu which allows you to interrupt block with an associated fatigue cost.
 - Lightning Blade Creation (C-rank Sealing) lost its interrupt draw capacity, and was moved down to C-rank, and is explicit about being self-only.
 - Fuuyoso Houin (B-rank Sealing) regained its ability to end the upkeeps of others, but is now limited to the user's elemental aptitudes, rather than being capable of sealing all five base elements.  It also only applies to effects which apply to others.
 - Moji Hyouki was revamped to allow for more direct application mid-combat, allowing for the creation of traps.
 - Sword of Blood Oath (B-rank Sealing) was reduced to B rank, from A, and is explicit about being self-only.
 - Tensha Fuuin (A-rank Sealing) lost its ability to activate techniques with an upkeep, and had its X variable maximum changed to your base CE bonus, not including Fatigue.

## Advancement
 - Housing has been added!  Like wounds, it links to another page (a google sheet) which has further details, but the rules for housing are detailed on the website.  Like Second Characters, you require 100 Overcap in order to acquire and access Housing, and need much more in order to fully furnish your house; it is intentional to take potentially many months to fully utilize it, so don't be too concerned about how little you have to start with or how much it appears you might want with a wishlist.
